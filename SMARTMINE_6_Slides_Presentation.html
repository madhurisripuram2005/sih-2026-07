    🔴 The Ground Reality in Pit Inversions

    During dense winter mornings and monsoon thermal inversions, particulate dust and moisture droplets reduce driver visual line-of-sight to 3 to 5 meters.

    • Optical Blindness: Standard cameras and human eyes cannot detect unlit equipment or foot workers.
    • LiDAR Scattering: Near-infrared laser pulses suffer backscatter and dispersion through dense water droplets.
    • GPS Pit Occlusion: Deep open benches degrade satellite geometry, causing multi-meter drift.
